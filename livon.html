Overview

A health and wellness operating system.

LivOn brings a person’s whole health picture into one place (wearables, workouts, sleep and recovery, blood work, body composition and nutrition) and uses AI to turn it into clear daily guidance that no single device can provide.

  • 01Syncs Whoop, Oura, Garmin, Fitbit and Strava, alongside blood tests, body scans, meals and calendar
  • 02Liv, a personal AI coach that knows your entire health picture, from last night’s sleep to your latest blood work
  • 03A daily readiness score with recommendations, training sessions and coaching plans
  • 04Blood test reports explained in plain English, and meals logged from a single photo
  • 05A full Health Report, with all your data stored in one place for a clean export
