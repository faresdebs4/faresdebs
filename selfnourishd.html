Overview

A brand website the founder runs entirely on her own.

Selfnourishd is a health and wellness brand that makes health information accessible to everyone through simple explanations and practical recommendations. I designed and built both the public website and the admin panel behind it.

  • 01A free, searchable library of 53 wellness guides, organised by pillar
  • 02Custom tools tailored to the brand: a symptom-based food decoder and a cycle sync guide
  • 03A curated shop and a business enquiries flow
  • 04An admin panel to edit any page, the brand colours and images, with no code
  • 05AI-assisted article drafting: upload a carousel and caption, and the draft is written for you
