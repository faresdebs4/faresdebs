Overview

Networking, quietly and with purpose.

Nexwork is an invite-only, AI-powered networking platform built for the Middle East. It connects professionals quietly and with purpose, whether they are forming connections, exploring opportunities or finding collaborators. I led the end-to-end product lifecycle of its core Communities feature.

  • 01Communities: segmented ecosystems such as university alumni networks and VC portfolios
  • 02Member management, three levels of visibility, and announcements from admins
  • 03AI-driven targeted networking with Nexa, scoped to one or several communities
  • 04Community analytics: engagement, connections between groups, and unmet demand
  • 05Go-to-market and partnership outreach for the UAE launch
