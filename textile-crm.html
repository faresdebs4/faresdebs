Overview

Bespoke software that runs a trading business.

A custom system built for a textile trading business. It replaces scattered spreadsheets with a single tool to generate and track orders, follow shipments and payments, and stay on top of key tasks, with an executive dashboard for the CEO.

  • 01Over 1,200 orders logged, tracked and searchable in one place
  • 02Delivery alerts that flag shipments due soon or overdue
  • 03Turnover and commission tracking by supplier, client and year
  • 04Executive dashboard and task pages for the CEO
  • 05Excel import and export on every page
